The Story Behind The Lens

Our story

Photography that feels personal, not staged.

Clicks By Wahab started as one photographer's love for catching the moments people almost miss — the in-between laugh, the quiet glance, the pure joy of a celebration mid-swing. That instinct has since grown into a full studio serving portraits, weddings, podcasts and birthdays across Lahore.

We keep our approach simple: show up prepared, stay unobtrusive when it matters, and bring a creative, considered eye to every frame — whether it's a solo portrait session or a full Walima night.

Every project, big or small, is treated with the same attention to detail and genuine care for the story being told.
